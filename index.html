MON · WELCOME9:02 AM · pinned

👋 Welcome our 6 newest members — start here

“Welcome in, everyone! Drop ONE comment below: your name, where you’re joining from, and the single result you want from the next 90 days. Keep it to one sentence — future you will thank present you. Then go read the pinned ‘Start Here’ post and introduce yourself in the Wins thread when you get your first small result, however small. We celebrate starts here, not just finishes.”

Every new member also gets a personal welcome message the same day (see the 3-message series below) — nobody joins to silence.

TUE · ACTIVATION QUESTION8:15 AM

The Tuesday Question

“Honest answers only: what is the ONE thing you keep restarting every month? No fixing, no advice yet — just name it. Tomorrow’s live call opens with the three most common answers from this thread, so your comment literally shapes the call.”

Why it works: members who post in week one are dramatically more likely to still be here in month three. The question is low-effort, personal, and tied to a real event.

WED · LIVE EVENT12:00 PM · 38 RSVPs

📅 Today: Live Working Session — bring one real problem

“Doors open 10 minutes early. Bring ONE problem you’re stuck on — we work real cases live, in the order comments arrived in yesterday’s thread. Can’t make it? Drop your problem in the comments before 11 AM and we’ll cover the top three on the recording. Replay + notes will be pinned here by 6 PM.”

You (the founder) run this call. We build the runway: announcement, reminders, RSVP thread, replay post and follow-up.

THU · MEMBER WIN SPOTLIGHT10:30 AM

🏆 Thursday Wins — say it out loud

“It’s Thursday Wins. Rules: one win, any size, posted before midnight. Shipped the thing? Booked the call? Just showed up three days in a row? It counts. Read two other people’s wins and reply to both — that reply is worth more than you think. We round up the best three in Friday’s recap so the whole community sees what progress actually looks like here.”

FRI · MEMBER RESCUEPrivate message · sent 1:1

🤝 The quiet rescue (you never see this — that’s the point)

“Hey — noticed you’ve been quiet the last couple of weeks and wanted to check in properly, not with a pitch. Everything okay? If life just got busy, here’s the 10-minute catch-up: Tuesday’s question thread + Thursday’s wins will get you current. If something in the community isn’t working for you, reply and tell me straight — I read every word. Either way, your seat is here when you’re ready.”

Every week we review the member list, flag who has gone quiet, and reach out like this — before quiet turns into cancelled.
